# Freight Cost & Carrier Performance Analytics

SQL, Python, and Power BI (DAX) analysis of a freight network: where the money goes, which carriers
deliver, and what moving volume would cost.

> **Data note: synthetic, with planted relationships.** 24,000 shipments across 8 fictional carriers
> and 15 Canadian lanes were generated by `src/generate_shipments.py`. Relationships such as "cheap carrier,
> weak on long lanes" were built in deliberately (listed in [`docs/data_model.md`](docs/data_model.md)).
> **The findings below show the method working, not how real freight behaves.** Not employer data.

## Business questions
1. Which carriers give the best balance of cost and on-time delivery?
2. Does the answer change by lane distance?
3. How much do origin dwell time and detention charges cost, and what do they do to service?
4. How does peak season change cost and service?
5. How exposed is spend to the diesel price?
6. If volume moved from the cheapest carrier to a reliable one on long lanes, what is the trade-off?

## Findings (from the synthetic data)
Dataset: 24,000 shipments, $36.1M spend, 87.3% on-time.

1. **The cheapest carrier is the weakest on long lanes.** Prairie Express has the lowest long-haul cost
   ($1.941/km) but delivers 66.5% on-time over 800 km versus 88.7% under 300 km.
2. **Dwell time drives lateness and cost.** Shipments with 6h+ origin dwell are 75.4% on-time versus 88.4% under 2h,
   and average $351 detention versus $0. Detention totals $1.37M (3.8% of freight spend).
3. **Peak season (Nov 15 - Dec 24) hurts service more than headline cost.** Cost per shipment is +3.7%,
   but detention per shipment doubles ($100 vs $50), dwell rises from 2.6h to 3.5h, and on-time falls to 81.9% from 88.2%.
4. **Fuel surcharge is forecastable.** Fuel surcharges are 13.8% of spend. The most expensive month (Sep 2026)
   had average diesel of 179 cpl versus 150 cpl in the cheapest month (Jun 2026).

**Scenario:** moving Prairie Express's 1,242 long-haul shipments to Summit Carriers costs about +$929K (+27.6%) and avoids
about 319 late shipments, roughly $2,900 per late shipment avoided. Assumptions: capacity is available, observed
cost and on-time rates hold at higher volume, pallet mix is unchanged, and late-delivery penalties are not valued.
So this is a trade-off to put in front of a decision-maker, not a recommendation.

## What this project shows, and what it does not
- Shows: star-schema modelling, SQL (joins, CTEs, window functions, conditional aggregation), a reproducible
  Python pipeline, DAX measures, and a scenario with stated assumptions.
- Does not show: analysis of real operations, handling of messy real data, or validated recommendations.

## Run it
```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
python src/generate_shipments.py   # writes data/*.csv
python src/run_analysis.py         # builds SQLite DB, runs sql/*.sql, writes output/
```

## Structure
```
src/generate_shipments.py   synthetic star-schema data
src/run_analysis.py         load -> SQL -> charts -> scenario -> findings
sql/                        seven analysis queries (SQLite)
dax/measures.dax            Power BI measures
docs/                       data model, how to swap in real diesel prices
output/                     charts, result tables, findings.md
```
